SmartNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Data controller: Bugra Hizal (individual developer) · Contact: bugra.hizal@hotmail.com

In short: Your notes, handwriting, PDFs, recordings and study data stay on your device. Only if you turn on "Claude and ChatGPT Connection" yourself are the names of the notebooks you choose and the text recognized from their pages uploaded to our server; turning it off deletes them. If you turn on "Cloud AI" yourself, the messages you write to the AI and the recognized text of the relevant page are passed to Claude (Anthropic) to generate a reply; we do not store this content. You sign in with an account to use the app; only the details needed to sign in (email or phone number, name, account ID) are stored, and your notes are not uploaded to your account. There is no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Data we collect

  • Account: Signing in is required to use the app. Only the details needed to create your account and recognize you (below) are stored.
  • SmartNotes has no advertising, analytics or tracking. Your content (notes, handwriting, PDFs, audio, flashcards) is not sent to our servers or to third parties; the exceptions are the optional sync you turn on yourself (see "Claude and ChatGPT connection" below) and Cloud AI (see "Cloud AI" below).
  • The app makes network requests only to sign in, refresh your session, sign out, delete your account and — if you turned them on — for sync, Cloud AI and Pro verification.

Account

  • You need to sign in with an account to use the app (including the free plan). Your notes are not tied to your account and stay on your device.
  • Sign-in methods (as offered in the app): Sign in with Apple; where offered, Google, email (one-time code) or phone number (one-time code by SMS).
  • What is stored:
    • depending on the method, your email address or phone number
    • your name, if Apple or Google provides it (this may be an Apple "Hide My Email" relay address)
    • an account ID
    • sign-in times
  • Where: These details are stored on the servers of our authentication provider, Supabase (region: Frankfurt, Germany (EU)). SMS codes are sent through an SMS provider, and Google sign-in happens on Google's own page. These providers process the details only to provide sign-in on our behalf.
  • On your device: Session keys are kept only in the Keychain on your device, only for this device, and are not synced to iCloud Keychain. If Sign in with Apple is not connected to the authentication service, its details stay only on your device and are not sent to a server.
  • Purpose: The details are used only to sign you in and to match your purchases to your account. We do not send marketing emails, and we do not sell or share the details.
  • Purchase identifier: If you are signed in, an identifier derived from your account ID is attached to App Store purchases. It contains no personal information and stays in Apple's purchase record.
  • Retention: Your account is kept until you delete it.
  • Deletion: "Account & Subscription → Delete Account" permanently deletes your account and sign-in details from the server and immediately from your device. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also remove the connection in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple. Your notes stay on your device; to keep using the app you need to sign in again.

Claude and ChatGPT connection (optional, off by default)

  • When: Only if you sign in with a server-connected account, turn on sync in "Account & Subscription → Claude and ChatGPT Connection" and choose notebooks.
  • What is uploaded: the names of the notebooks you choose; page numbers and the lines of text recognized from your handwriting; suggestions left by Claude or ChatGPT and their status (pending, added, rejected). Pen strokes, images, audio recordings, PDF files, notebooks you did not choose and locked notebooks are not uploaded (locking a notebook deletes its cloud copy).
  • Pro verification: While sync is on, the App Store transaction records on your device (signed by Apple; they contain the product, dates and transaction number, not payment details) are sent to the server to verify that you may leave suggestions on pages. The server stores only the Pro status, product, expiry date and original transaction number.
  • Where: On Supabase servers (region: Frankfurt, Germany (EU)), over an encrypted connection; the data is linked to your account and, through row-level security, can be read only with your session.
  • Claude and ChatGPT: You set up the connection in Claude or ChatGPT yourself and grant access with your SmartNotes account. The AI you authorize can read and search the synced text and leave suggestions on your pages; it cannot change your page, suggestions are added only when you tap "Add to Page", and they are still checked by the on-device math engine. Text sent to these services is governed by Anthropic's and OpenAI's own privacy policies.
  • Deletion: Turning sync off immediately deletes the cloud copy (notebook names, page text, pending suggestions); removing a notebook from the selection deletes that notebook's copy. "Delete Account" deletes all of this and the Pro verification record as well. You can revoke the access you granted to Claude or ChatGPT in their settings.

Cloud AI (Claude; optional, off by default)

  • When: Only if you sign in with a server account and confirm "Account & Subscription → AI → Cloud AI (Claude)" or "Turn On Cloud AI" in AI Chat. You can turn it off anytime in the same place.
  • What is sent: in AI Chat, the messages you write, the earlier messages of that chat and the text recognized from the open page's handwriting; in AI Pencil, the text you selected; in Analysis and Study, the page's recognized text and the question you ask in Analysis. Pen strokes, images, audio recordings and PDF files are not sent. Content of locked notebooks is never sent.
  • Where: The request travels over an encrypted connection through our server (Supabase, Frankfurt, Germany (EU)) to Anthropic's Claude API, and the reply comes back to you. Anthropic processes API data on our behalf under its own commercial terms and privacy policy.
  • What we keep: We do not store or log the content (messages, note text, replies). To limit abuse and cost, we keep only this record linked to your account: time of the request, feature (chat, pencil, analysis, study), model used, token counts and estimated cost. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Limits: The number of requests per day depends on your plan; when the limit is reached or you're offline, AI keeps working with the on-device model.
  • AI replies: They are suggestions. Nothing is written on your page until you tap "Add to Page".

On-device processing

Handwriting recognition, live math, AI features (AI Pencil, AI Chat, analysis, summaries, flashcards, "Ask Your Notes") and image generation use Apple frameworks that run on your device when Cloud AI is off (Vision, Apple Intelligence on-device models, Image Playground). In that case your content is not sent to us. Apple's own processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostic reports

If the app crashes or slows down, iOS provides a measurement report at most once a day (Apple MetricKit: crash type, call stack, launch time, memory and battery metrics). SmartNotes keeps these reports only on your device (up to 30) and never sends them anywhere. They contain no notes, handwriting or account details. Only you can choose to send them to us with "Account & Subscription → Share Diagnostic Reports", and you can delete them with "Delete Reports".

Calendar

"Add to Calendar" finds dated lines on your page on the device (e.g. "Friday 3 pm meeting") and suggests them in the system's "Add Event" screen. iOS adds the event only if you confirm it. SmartNotes does not read your calendar and does not send calendar information anywhere.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you start an audio recording, to sync lecture or meeting audio with your notes. Recordings are saved only on your device and can be deleted at any time.

Translation

"Translate Selected Text" and "Translate" on a text box open Apple's system translation; only you start it. Depending on your settings, Apple translates the text on your device or sends it to Apple for processing (iOS tells you the first time; you can choose on-device translation in Settings). SmartNotes does not send the translated text to us. Apple's processing is subject to the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notebook lock

A notebook you lock opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. iOS performs the check; no face, fingerprint or passcode data reaches the app. The lock and the encryption key of locked notebooks stay on your device only.

Photos

When you add an image, you choose it in the system photo picker; the app only receives the image you selected and stores a copy inside your page. If you save an exported page to Photos, only "add" permission is requested; the app does not read your photo library.

Purchases and payment

Subscriptions, free trials and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Your payment method (card, etc.) stays in your Apple Account; SmartNotes never sees or stores your card or payment details. Apple shares only the purchase status (plan, trial, expiry date) with the app so it can unlock Pro features.

Backups and iCloud

Backups you create are written to a folder you choose (for example in iCloud Drive). If iCloud sync is added in the future, your data will be stored in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Children

SmartNotes is suitable for students. An account is required to use it; if you are under the age of digital consent in your country (for example 13 or 16), create an account only with a parent's permission. If you learn that a child created an account without permission, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because your notes are on your device, you can view, export and delete them directly in the app. In "Account & Subscription" you can see what is stored for your account and delete all of it with "Delete Account". For rights under the laws where you live (for example access, correction, deletion and objection under the GDPR), write to us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: bugra.hizal@hotmail.com

"Bulut AI"yı kendin açarsan, AI'ya yazdığın mesaj ve ilgili sayfanın tanınmış metni yanıt üretmek için Claude'a (Anthropic) iletilir; biz bu içeriği saklamayız. Uygulamayı kullanmak için hesapla giriş yaparsın; yalnızca giriş için gereken bilgiler (e-posta veya telefon, ad, hesap kimliği) saklanır, notların hesaba yüklenmez. Reklam, analitik veya izleme yoktur.

  • SmartNotes'ta reklam, analitik veya izleme yoktur. İçeriğin (notlar, el yazısı, PDF, ses, kartlar) sunucularımıza ya da üçüncü taraflara gönderilmez; istisnalar, kendin açtığın isteğe bağlı eşitleme (aşağıda "Claude ve ChatGPT bağlantısı") ve bulut AI'dır (aşağıda "Bulut AI").

    • seçtiğin yönteme göre e-posta adresin veya telefon numaran

Apple ile giriş yaptıysan bağlantıyı Ayarlar → adın → Apple ile Giriş bölümünden de kaldırabilirsin. Notların cihazında kalır; uygulamayı kullanmaya devam etmek için yeniden giriş yapman gerekir.

Cihazda işleme

El yazısı tanıma, canlı matematik, yapay zekâ özellikleri (AI Kalem, AI Sohbet, analiz, özetler, bilgi kartları, "Notlarına Sor") ve görsel üretimi, bulut AI kapalıyken cihazında çalışan Apple çerçevelerini kullanır (Vision, Apple Intelligence cihaz üstü modelleri, Image Playground). Bu durumda içeriğin bize gönderilmez. Apple'ın kendi işlemleri Apple Gizlilik Politikası'na tabidir.

Tanılama raporları

iOS, uygulama çökerse veya yavaşlarsa günde en fazla bir kez ölçüm raporu verir (Apple MetricKit: çökme türü, çağrı yığını, açılış süresi, bellek ve pil ölçümleri). SmartNotes bu raporları yalnızca cihazında saklar (en fazla 30 rapor) ve hiçbir yere göndermez. Raporlarda notların, el yazın veya hesap bilgilerin yoktur. "Hesap ve Abonelik → Tanılama Raporlarını Paylaş" ile bize göndermeyi yalnızca sen seçebilir, "Raporları Sil" ile silebilirsin.

Takvim

"Takvime Ekle", sayfandaki tarihli satırları (ör. "Cuma 15.00 toplantı") cihazda bulur ve sistemin "Etkinlik Ekle" ekranında önerir. Etkinliği sen onaylarsan iOS takvimine ekler. SmartNotes takvimini okumaz ve takvim bilgisini hiçbir yere göndermez.

Mikrofon

Mikrofon yalnızca ses kaydı başlattığında, ders veya toplantı sesini notlarınla eşzamanlamak için kullanılır. Kayıtlar yalnızca cihazında saklanır ve istediğin zaman silinebilir.

Çeviri

"Seçili Metni Çevir" ve metin kutusundaki "Çevir", Apple'ın sistem çevirisini açar; çeviriyi yalnızca sen başlatırsın. Apple, ayarına göre metni cihazında çevirir ya da işlemek için Apple'a gönderir (ilk kullanımda iOS bunu bildirir; cihazda çeviriyi Ayarlar'dan seçebilirsin). SmartNotes çevrilen metni bize göndermez. Apple'ın işlemleri Apple Gizlilik Politikası'na tabidir.

Defter kilidi

Kilitlediğin defter Face ID, Touch ID veya cihaz parolasıyla açılır. Doğrulamayı iOS yapar; yüz, parmak izi veya parola bilgisi uygulamaya gelmez. Kilit bilgisi ve kilitli defterlerin şifreleme anahtarı yalnızca cihazındadır.

Fotoğraflar

Görsel eklerken sistemin fotoğraf seçicisinde seçimi sen yaparsın; uygulama yalnızca seçtiğin görseli alır ve sayfanın içinde bir kopyasını saklar. Dışa aktardığın bir sayfayı Fotoğraflar'a kaydedersen yalnızca "ekleme" izni istenir; uygulama fotoğraf arşivini okumaz.

Satın almalar ve ödeme

Abonelikler, ücretsiz denemeler ve ömür boyu satın alma Apple tarafından App Store üzerinden işlenir. Ödeme yöntemin (kart vb.) Apple hesabında durur; SmartNotes kart veya ödeme bilgini görmez ve saklamaz. Apple, Pro özelliklerin açılması için uygulamayla yalnızca satın alma durumunu (plan, deneme, bitiş tarihi) paylaşır.

Yedekler ve iCloud

Oluşturduğun yedekler senin seçtiğin klasöre (örneğin iCloud Drive) yazılır. İleride iCloud eşitleme eklenirse verilerin, bizim erişemediğimiz kendi özel iCloud hesabında saklanır.

Çocuklar

SmartNotes öğrencilere uygundur. Kullanmak için hesap gerekir; bulunduğun ülkedeki dijital rıza yaşının (örneğin 13 veya 16) altındaysan hesabı yalnızca ebeveyninin izniyle aç. Bir çocuğun izinsiz hesap açtığını fark edersen bize yaz; hesabı sileriz.

Hakların

Hesap bilgilerin için "Hesap ve Abonelik" ekranında neyin saklandığını görebilir ve "Hesabı Sil" ile hepsini silebilirsin. Bulunduğun yerin mevzuatından doğan haklar (örneğin KVKK, GDPR kapsamında erişim, düzeltme, silme, itiraz) için bize yazabilirsin.
